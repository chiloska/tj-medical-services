Clinic fact sheet

Everything about TJ Medical Services on one page: services, prices, hours, locations, travel, policies and answers to common questions. This page is also the knowledge source for our virtual receptionist.

About the clinic

TJ Medical Services is a bilingual (English and Spanish) medical clinic for patients from the United States and Canada. Procedures happen at our clinic in Zona Río, Tijuana, Baja California, Mexico. Our office in San Ysidro, San Diego, California handles consultations, pre-op and post-op visits and transport to the clinic. Prices are typically 40% to 70% below U.S. prices.

Services

Dental care and implants

Dental care is the most common reason patients cross to Tijuana, and it is our largest department. Our dentists plan every case with a 3D cone-beam CT scan and an intraoral scan, and crowns are milled in our own lab, so most single crowns are ready in 2 to 3 days. Implant brands offered: Neodent, Straumann and Nobel Biocare. Your quote names the brand, the crown material and the number of visits.

Treatments: Exams, cleanings and X-rays; Fillings, root canals and extractions (including wisdom teeth); Single implants with abutment and crown; All-on-4 and All-on-6 full-arch implants; Zirconia and porcelain crowns and bridges; Porcelain veneers and teeth whitening; Full and partial dentures; Bone grafts and sinus lifts.

Crowns and veneers: 1 trip of 3–5 days. Implants: 2 trips, 3–6 months apart (placement, then final crown). All-on-4: surgery and temporary teeth in 3–5 days, final teeth on a second trip 4–6 months later.

Weight-loss surgery

Our bariatric program combines laparoscopic surgery with pre-surgery testing and 12 months of follow-up with a bilingual nutritionist by video call. Typical candidates have a BMI of 35 or higher, or 30 or higher with a weight-related condition such as type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea. Every candidate completes a medical questionnaire and is reviewed by the surgeon before a date is confirmed.

Treatments: Gastric sleeve (vertical sleeve gastrectomy); Roux-en-Y gastric bypass; Mini gastric bypass; Revision surgery (band-to-sleeve, sleeve-to-bypass); Endoscopic gastric balloon (non-surgical).

Plan on 3 to 4 days in Tijuana: arrival and testing, surgery, 1–2 nights in hospital, then a check-up before you travel home.

Cosmetic and plastic surgery

Our plastic surgeons are certified by the Mexican Council of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery (CMCPER). Procedures take place in our accredited operating rooms with a board-certified anesthesiologist. Every package includes compression garments, post-op lymphatic massage sessions and a recovery-hotel stay with nurse visits.

Treatments: Breast augmentation, lift and reduction; Tummy tuck (abdominoplasty); Liposuction and Brazilian butt lift (BBL); Mommy makeover; Rhinoplasty; Eyelid surgery (blepharoplasty) and facelift.

Plan on 7 to 10 days in the Tijuana–San Diego area. Most body procedures need a 1-night hospital stay, then recovery-hotel nights with daily nurse checks.

Orthopedics and joint replacement

Our orthopedic surgeons perform joint replacement and arthroscopic surgery using implants from established U.S. and European manufacturers. Your quote names the implant model. Joint-replacement patients start physical therapy the day after surgery and continue daily sessions until they are cleared to travel. We send your full records and a home-exercise plan to your local doctor or physical therapist.

Treatments: Total knee replacement; Total hip replacement; Knee arthroscopy and meniscus repair; ACL reconstruction; Shoulder arthroscopy and rotator-cuff repair; Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) joint injections.

Joint replacement: plan on 10 to 14 days. Arthroscopy: 4 to 5 days.

Check-ups, imaging and lab tests

Many patients come for a full check-up in a single morning. Results are reviewed with an internist the same day and delivered as a written report in English or Spanish. Imaging and lab tests can be booked without a doctor's order, although we recommend bringing one if your own doctor asked for a specific study.

Treatments: Executive check-up (labs, ECG, chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound, internist consult); MRI and CT scans; Ultrasound and echocardiogram; Mammography and bone-density scan; Colonoscopy and upper endoscopy; Full blood panels.

Most check-ups and imaging take a single morning; colonoscopy requires prep the day before.

Specialist consultations

We schedule specialist consultations, often within the same week, in English or Spanish. Specialists can order tests on the same day so you leave with a diagnosis and a written plan.

Treatments: Cardiology; Gastroenterology; Dermatology and non-surgical aesthetics (Botox, fillers); Gynecology; Urology; Ophthalmology, including cataract and LASIK evaluation; Internal medicine.

A consultation takes about 45 minutes; allow half a day if tests are added.

Booking process

1) The patient calls, messages on WhatsApp, emails or sends the website form. 2) A coordinator sends a medical questionnaire and collects X-rays, photos or lab results. 3) A doctor reviews the case and the patient receives a written, itemized quote within 2 business days; surgery candidates also get a free video consultation with the surgeon. 4) A refundable deposit reserves the date. 5) The coordinator books transport and hotel and sends a day-by-day itinerary.

Travel and border crossing

Most patients fly into San Diego International Airport (SAN). Surgery patients are picked up at SAN arrivals. Patients can also fly into Tijuana International Airport (TIJ) or use the Cross Border Xpress (CBX) bridge. A free shuttle runs from the San Diego office to the Tijuana clinic every hour on the hour, 7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday; seats must be reserved. The San Diego office is about 5 minutes from the San Ysidro port of entry, and the clinic is about 15 minutes by car from the border. Eligible patients get a free medical quick-crossing pass from the City of Tijuana for the medical lane at San Ysidro and Otay Mesa when returning to the U.S.; the clinic never sells passes, and lane hours are set by the city. U.S. citizens need a passport, passport card, or SENTRI/Global Entry card to return to the U.S.; Canadians need a passport. Tijuana is on Pacific Time.

Payments, deposits and insurance

Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit cards, wire transfer, cash in USD or MXN; no card surcharge; third-party financing subject to approval. Deposits: $500 for surgery, $200 for dental treatment over $2,000, none for consultations, check-ups or imaging. Deposits are fully refunded with 14 or more days' notice or if the doctor finds the procedure unsafe; with less notice, the deposit can be moved once to a date within 12 months. Refunds take up to 10 business days. The clinic does not bill insurance directly but provides itemized invoices with procedure codes for out-of-network claims. Guarantees: 12 months on corrective procedures related to surgery; 5 years on dental implant fixtures; 2 years on crowns and veneers.

Rules for the virtual receptionist

  • If a caller describes chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, fainting, signs of stroke, or any emergency: tell them to hang up and call 911 now (911 works in the U.S. and Mexico).
  • Post-operative patients with urgent but non-emergency concerns (fever, wound changes, nausea): transfer to the on-call nurse.
  • Never diagnose, recommend a specific treatment, say whether someone is a candidate, or give medication advice. Offer a free consultation or a nurse callback instead.
  • Always describe prices as starting prices and explain that the final price comes in a written quote.
  • Never promise a specific medical-lane wait time, crossing time or result of a procedure.
  • To book: book the consultation or evaluation directly in the calendar (name and phone number required, email optional) and tell the caller a WhatsApp confirmation will arrive shortly. Reschedules and cancellations are also confirmed by WhatsApp.
  • If the caller asks for the clinic or office address, offer to send it by WhatsApp to the number they are calling from, and only send it if they agree.
  • Answer in the caller's language (English or Spanish). If a caller asks for a person, transfer them or take a callback request.

Frequently asked questions

Is TJ Medical Services in Tijuana or San Diego?

Both. Procedures take place at our clinic in Zona Río, Tijuana. Our San Diego office in San Ysidro, about 5 minutes from the border, handles consultations, pre-op appointments, post-op checks and the shuttle to the clinic.

Do the doctors and staff speak English?

Yes. All doctors, nurses and coordinators speak English and Spanish, and all written documents are available in both languages.

How much can I save compared with the United States or Canada?

Most patients pay roughly 40% to 70% less than typical U.S. prices for the same procedure. Your exact price is confirmed in a written quote after your doctor reviews your case.

Are the prices on the website final?

No. Website prices are starting prices in U.S. dollars. Your final price depends on your medical history, the materials chosen and what your doctor recommends, and is always given in a written quote before you travel. The quoted price does not change once you accept it unless your treatment plan changes and you approve the change.

What is included in a surgery package?

Surgery packages include the surgeon and anesthesiologist fees, operating room, hospital stay, pre-surgery lab tests, medications during your stay, recovery-hotel nights, nurse visits, ground transportation from San Diego airport or our San Diego office, and follow-up calls. Flights, meals outside the hospital and travel insurance are not included.

Do you accept U.S. or Canadian insurance?

We do not bill insurance companies directly. We give you an itemized invoice in English with procedure codes so you can file an out-of-network claim with your insurer. Some U.S. plans with cross-border networks may reimburse part of the cost; check with your plan first.

How do I pay?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit cards, bank wire transfer and cash in U.S. dollars or Mexican pesos. Surgery requires a deposit to reserve your date and the balance on the day of your pre-op appointment. Third-party patient financing is available, subject to lender approval.

How much is the deposit and is it refundable?

The deposit is $500 for surgery and $200 for dental treatment over $2,000. It is fully refundable if you cancel 14 or more days before your date, or if your doctor decides the procedure is not safe for you. It can be moved once to a new date at no cost.

What documents do I need to cross the border?

To return to the United States, U.S. citizens need a valid U.S. passport, passport card, or a trusted-traveler card such as SENTRI or Global Entry. Canadian citizens need a valid passport. Other visitors need a passport and any U.S. visa required for their nationality. Check current requirements with U.S. Customs and Border Protection before traveling.

Do you help with the border wait on the way back?

Yes. Eligible patients receive a medical quick-crossing pass issued by the City of Tijuana, which gives access to the medical lane at the San Ysidro and Otay Mesa ports of entry. Passes are free for our patients and are only issued to people treated at our clinic. Lane hours are set by the city and can change; your coordinator confirms them before your return.

Do you pick me up?

Yes. Surgery packages include pickup at San Diego International Airport (SAN). All patients can use our free shuttle from the San Diego office in San Ysidro to the Tijuana clinic. Dental and check-up patients can also walk across the border; the clinic is about 15 minutes by car from the San Ysidro crossing.

Can I fly into Tijuana instead?

Yes. If you fly into Tijuana International Airport (TIJ), we pick you up there. Travelers coming from the U.S. side can also use the Cross Border Xpress (CBX) bridge, which connects a terminal in San Diego directly to the Tijuana airport; a CBX ticket is required.

Is it safe?

Our clinic operates under a license from COFEPRIS, Mexico's federal health regulator, and our specialists are certified by their Mexican specialty boards. Patients travel with our drivers between the airport, the San Diego office, the clinic and partner hotels in Zona Río. As in any large city, we recommend avoiding travel at night in unfamiliar areas.

How long do I need to stay?

It depends on the treatment: a check-up takes one morning, crowns or veneers 3 to 5 days, weight-loss surgery 3 to 4 days, cosmetic surgery 7 to 10 days and joint replacement 10 to 14 days. Implants and All-on-4 require a second trip a few months later.

Can I bring a companion?

Yes, and we recommend it for any surgery. One companion can stay in your recovery-hotel room at no extra cost and ride in our transport.

What happens if there is a complication after I go home?

You get a 24/7 on-call number for questions during recovery. Follow-up checks can be done at our San Diego office or by video call. Surgical work includes a written guarantee that covers corrective procedures related to the original surgery for 12 months; dental implants carry a 5-year guarantee on the implant fixture. For an emergency, call 911 immediately, in the U.S. or in Mexico.

Can I get my medical records?

Yes. You receive a digital copy of your records, imaging and operative report in English at discharge, and we can send them to your doctor at home.

How do I get a quote?

Call or message us, or send the request form on our contact page. A coordinator sends a medical questionnaire, a doctor reviews it along with any X-rays or photos you share, and you receive a written quote within 2 business days. Surgery candidates also get a free video consultation with their surgeon.

Do you treat children?

Our check-up, imaging and specialist services see patients 16 and older, and general dentistry sees children over 6 with a parent or guardian present. We do not perform cosmetic or weight-loss surgery on patients under 18.

Can the virtual receptionist give medical advice?

No. Our virtual receptionist answers questions about services, prices, travel and appointments, and passes medical questions to a nurse or doctor. It cannot diagnose, recommend treatment or adjust medication.
