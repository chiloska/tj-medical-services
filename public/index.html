What we treat

  • Dental care and implants

    Implants, All-on-4, crowns, veneers and full-mouth restorations, with 3D CBCT scanning on site.

  • Weight-loss surgery

    Laparoscopic gastric sleeve, gastric bypass and revisions, with a 12-month nutrition program.

  • Cosmetic and plastic surgery

    Breast, body and face procedures by surgeons certified by the Mexican board of plastic surgery.

  • Orthopedics and joint replacement

    Knee and hip replacement, arthroscopy and sports injuries, with physical therapy before you fly home.

  • Check-ups, imaging and lab tests

    Same-day executive check-ups, MRI, CT, ultrasound, mammography and colonoscopy, with results in English.

  • Specialist consultations

    Cardiology, gastroenterology, dermatology, gynecology, urology and ophthalmology, often within the week.

How a visit works

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, message us on WhatsApp or send the form. We reply within one business day.

  2. Doctor review and written quote

    A doctor reviews your questionnaire, X-rays or photos. You get an itemized quote within 2 business days.

  3. Reserve your date

    A refundable deposit holds your date. Your coordinator books transport and your recovery hotel.

  4. Treatment in Tijuana

    We pick you up in San Diego and drive you to the clinic. A coordinator is with you at every appointment.

  5. Home, with follow-up

    You cross back using the medical lane pass, then check in with us in San Diego or by video.

Two locations, one coordinator

Your coordinator plans everything from our San Diego office and stays your single point of contact from the first call to your last follow-up.
