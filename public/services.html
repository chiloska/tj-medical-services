Services

Six departments under one roof in Zona Río, Tijuana. Every treatment is planned with your doctor before you travel.

  • Dental care and implants
  • Weight-loss surgery
  • Cosmetic and plastic surgery
  • Orthopedics and joint replacement
  • Check-ups, imaging and lab tests
  • Specialist consultations

Dental care and implants

Dental care is the most common reason patients cross to Tijuana, and it is our largest department. Our dentists plan every case with a 3D cone-beam CT scan and an intraoral scan, and crowns are milled in our own lab, so most single crowns are ready in 2 to 3 days.

Implant brands offered: Neodent, Straumann and Nobel Biocare. Your quote names the brand, the crown material and the number of visits.

Treatments

  • Exams, cleanings and X-rays
  • Fillings, root canals and extractions (including wisdom teeth)
  • Single implants with abutment and crown
  • All-on-4 and All-on-6 full-arch implants
  • Zirconia and porcelain crowns and bridges
  • Porcelain veneers and teeth whitening
  • Full and partial dentures
  • Bone grafts and sinus lifts

Time in Tijuana

Crowns and veneers: 1 trip of 3–5 days. Implants: 2 trips, 3–6 months apart (placement, then final crown). All-on-4: surgery and temporary teeth in 3–5 days, final teeth on a second trip 4–6 months later.

Weight-loss surgery

Our bariatric program combines laparoscopic surgery with pre-surgery testing and 12 months of follow-up with a bilingual nutritionist by video call.

Typical candidates have a BMI of 35 or higher, or 30 or higher with a weight-related condition such as type 2 diabetes or sleep apnea. Every candidate completes a medical questionnaire and is reviewed by the surgeon before a date is confirmed.

Treatments

  • Gastric sleeve (vertical sleeve gastrectomy)
  • Roux-en-Y gastric bypass
  • Mini gastric bypass
  • Revision surgery (band-to-sleeve, sleeve-to-bypass)
  • Endoscopic gastric balloon (non-surgical)

Time in Tijuana

Plan on 3 to 4 days in Tijuana: arrival and testing, surgery, 1–2 nights in hospital, then a check-up before you travel home.

Starting prices for weight-loss surgery

Cosmetic and plastic surgery

Our plastic surgeons are certified by the Mexican Council of Plastic, Aesthetic and Reconstructive Surgery (CMCPER). Procedures take place in our accredited operating rooms with a board-certified anesthesiologist.

Every package includes compression garments, post-op lymphatic massage sessions and a recovery-hotel stay with nurse visits.

Treatments

  • Breast augmentation, lift and reduction
  • Tummy tuck (abdominoplasty)
  • Liposuction and Brazilian butt lift (BBL)
  • Mommy makeover
  • Rhinoplasty
  • Eyelid surgery (blepharoplasty) and facelift

Time in Tijuana

Plan on 7 to 10 days in the Tijuana–San Diego area. Most body procedures need a 1-night hospital stay, then recovery-hotel nights with daily nurse checks.

Starting prices for cosmetic and plastic surgery

Orthopedics and joint replacement

Our orthopedic surgeons perform joint replacement and arthroscopic surgery using implants from established U.S. and European manufacturers. Your quote names the implant model.

Joint-replacement patients start physical therapy the day after surgery and continue daily sessions until they are cleared to travel. We send your full records and a home-exercise plan to your local doctor or physical therapist.

Treatments

  • Total knee replacement
  • Total hip replacement
  • Knee arthroscopy and meniscus repair
  • ACL reconstruction
  • Shoulder arthroscopy and rotator-cuff repair
  • Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) joint injections

Time in Tijuana

Joint replacement: plan on 10 to 14 days. Arthroscopy: 4 to 5 days.

Starting prices for orthopedics and joint replacement

Check-ups, imaging and lab tests

Many patients come for a full check-up in a single morning. Results are reviewed with an internist the same day and delivered as a written report in English or Spanish.

Imaging and lab tests can be booked without a doctor's order, although we recommend bringing one if your own doctor asked for a specific study.

Treatments

  • Executive check-up (labs, ECG, chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound, internist consult)
  • MRI and CT scans
  • Ultrasound and echocardiogram
  • Mammography and bone-density scan
  • Colonoscopy and upper endoscopy
  • Full blood panels

Time in Tijuana

Most check-ups and imaging take a single morning; colonoscopy requires prep the day before.

Starting prices for check-ups, imaging and lab tests

Specialist consultations

We schedule specialist consultations, often within the same week, in English or Spanish. Specialists can order tests on the same day so you leave with a diagnosis and a written plan.

Treatments

  • Cardiology
  • Gastroenterology
  • Dermatology and non-surgical aesthetics (Botox, fillers)
  • Gynecology
  • Urology
  • Ophthalmology, including cataract and LASIK evaluation
  • Internal medicine

Time in Tijuana

A consultation takes about 45 minutes; allow half a day if tests are added.

Starting prices for specialist consultations
