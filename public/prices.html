Starting prices

Prices are in U.S. dollars and show where each treatment starts. Your exact price comes in a written quote after a doctor reviews your case, and it does not change once you accept it.

What surgery packages include

  • Surgeon, anesthesiologist and operating-room fees
  • Pre-surgery lab tests, ECG and chest X-ray
  • Hospital stay and medications while admitted
  • Recovery-hotel nights with nurse visits (room shared with one companion)
  • Pickup at San Diego (SAN) or Tijuana (TIJ) airport and all local transport
  • Medical-lane crossing pass for your return, when eligible
  • Follow-up calls and visits at our San Diego office

Not included: flights, meals outside the hospital, travel insurance and extra hotel nights you request.
