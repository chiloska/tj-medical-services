Payments and policies

How to pay, how deposits and cancellations work, what our guarantees cover, and how we handle your information.

Payment methods

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit cards, bank wire transfer, and cash in U.S. dollars or Mexican pesos. Card payments have no surcharge. Patient financing is available through third-party lenders, subject to their approval; your coordinator can send the application link.

Deposits

  • Surgery: $500 deposit to reserve your date; balance due at your pre-op appointment.
  • Dental treatment over $2,000: $200 deposit; each phase is paid when it is completed.
  • Consultations, check-ups and imaging: no deposit; pay on the day.

Cancellations and changes

  • 14 or more days before your date: full deposit refund.
  • Fewer than 14 days: the deposit can be moved once to a new date within 12 months, but is not refunded.
  • If your doctor decides the procedure is not safe for you, your deposit is refunded in full.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days.

Insurance

We do not bill insurance companies directly. You receive an itemized invoice in English with procedure codes to submit an out-of-network claim. Check with your insurer before traveling; most U.S. and Canadian plans do not cover elective treatment abroad.

Guarantees

  • Surgery: corrective procedures related to the original surgery are covered for 12 months (surgeon and facility fees); travel is not covered.
  • Dental implants: 5 years on the implant fixture; crowns and veneers: 2 years against breakage under normal use.
  • Guarantees require attending the follow-up appointments in your care plan.

Privacy

We handle your personal and medical information under Mexico's federal personal-data protection law and only share it with the doctors treating you, or with others you authorize in writing. Calls to our phone line may be answered by an AI virtual receptionist and recorded to improve service; you can ask for a person at any time.
